BYJAY started as a personal passion project — I've always loved collecting pins and Italian charm bracelets, and I wanted to share that with everyone who has a fandom, an aesthetic, or just wants to wear something that says them.

Every piece in the shop is handpicked by me. I look for quality, detail, and most importantly — personality. Whether you're into F1, Disney, Sanrio, or just cool vibes, there's something here for you.

Based in Manila 📍 and shipping nationwide. DM me anytime on Instagram or TikTok — I respond fast and I'm happy to help you find the perfect piece or build a custom order.
